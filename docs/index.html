Stoppo

One stop at a time.

A calm transit puzzle. Every car rides only the line of its own color, one stop at a time, into an empty station. Shuffle them aside and bring Stoppo, the car with the eyes, home to the ringed station.

Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email hello@stefanoimparato.com and include your device model and the level number if it's about a specific puzzle.

Common questions

How do I move a car? Drag it along its line, or tap an empty station to send the only car that can reach it. If more than one car can go there, tap the car you want first.

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every level has been checked by a solver, and the move count shown is the fewest possible.

Does my progress sync? Yes, between your iPhone and iPad when you're signed in to the same iCloud account.
