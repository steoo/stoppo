Stoppo

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Stoppo does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores

To remember your progress, Stoppo saves the level you are on and your best move count for each level. This is stored:

  • On your device, in the app's own storage. It is included in your device backups like any other app data.
  • In your iCloud account, if you are signed in to iCloud, so your progress syncs between your own devices. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The data stays in your iCloud account; the developer cannot see or access it.

This progress contains no personal information: just level numbers and move counts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal data.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not need an internet connection to play.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the progress stored on your device. To remove the copy in iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone or iPad and delete Stoppo's data, or turn off iCloud for the app.

Children

Stoppo is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
