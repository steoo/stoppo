How to play

Each car can only ride the subway line of its own color.

Tap a car to see where it can go, then tap a highlighted station. A car moves one stop at a time, and only into an empty station.

Get the starred car to the ringed station of the same color. Other cars will be in the way — shuffle them around!

Solve in the fewest moves to match par.
