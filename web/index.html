Bring Stoppo home. Stoppo is the car with the eyes. Get it into the ringed station.

Every car rides only the line of its own color, one stop at a time, and only into an empty station.

Drag a car along its line, or tap an empty station to send the car that can reach it. If more than one can, tap the car first. Other cars are in the way — move them aside.

Each level shows the fewest moves possible. Can you match it?
